Nano Digital Twin

THE QUESTION

Can we build a virtual copy of Downtown Brooklyn's streets, so computer-vision models for accident detection and evacuation routing can be tested in simulation first?

WHAT I DID

  • Built the CARLA-modified Unreal Engine and the CARLA C++ simulation core from source.
  • Connected the C++ core to Python with pybind11, so teammates could spawn vehicles, set the weather and read camera frames from Python scripts.
  • Built a simplified street map of the area in Unreal Engine.
  • Fixed system-level problems (missing DLLs, MSVC version mismatches) and wrote troubleshooting docs for the team.

WHERE IT STANDS

The simulation environment and a first map were done. The next step, feeding simulated camera images to the vision models, was beyond my time on the project.

Work-zone Safety for Self-driving Cars

THE QUESTION

Construction work zones are some of the hardest places to drive: lanes shift, cones appear and workers walk on the road. How safely do autonomous-driving agents handle them?

WHAT I DID

  • Designed custom CARLA scenarios for a work-zone driving-safety benchmark, such as pedestrian behavior models.
  • Studied reinforcement-learning methods (PPO, TD3, SAC) for driving agents.

Cities and Depression: Urban Scaling

THE QUESTION

Bigger cities have more of almost everything, from GDP to crime. Does depression grow faster or slower than population, and which city factors actually drive it?

WHAT I DO

  • Extend a peer-reviewed urban-scaling study of depression across U.S. metro areas, with a pandas pipeline over four government data sources.
  • Add commute time and commute mode as new predictors.
  • Use eigenvector spatial filtering to account for neighboring cities affecting each other.
  • Use the PC algorithm (causal discovery) to map which factors are directly linked to depression.

ShopScout: Accessible Self-checkout

THE QUESTION

Self-checkout machines are built around touchscreens, which makes them hard to use for blind and low-vision shoppers. Can an assistive tool guide them through checkout on their own?

WHAT I DO

  • Review research on how blind and low-vision people use self-checkout, touchscreen kiosks and physical device interfaces.
  • Compare models for real-time guidance: fast object detectors (YOLO) that can run on an edge device like a Jetson, versus vision-language models that understand new items and read text but take seconds to answer.

Offshore Wind Turbine Maintenance

THE QUESTION

Repairing an offshore wind turbine means sending a crew out to sea, limited by weather and boats, while blade defects keep getting worse. When is the best time to repair, and does adding preventive maintenance change the answer?

WHAT I DO

  • Build on a framework (Haensch et al., 2025) that models blade defects worsening from minor to severe to replacement as a Markov chain, and weighs cost against worker safety with Cumulative Prospect Theory.
  • Add preventive maintenance to the possible actions, and model how each action changes the chance that a defect gets worse.
  • Turn failure rates from the literature into transition probabilities.
